Bundled sprite art — "Custom skin" sprite library
===================================================

These sprites are the bundled 2D art the player can equip in the custom-skin
line (shop → "Custom skin"). Every sprite in this directory is licensed
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use in any
project, including commercial ones, no permission required, attribution not
required (but appreciated, hence this file).

Source: FreeGameSprites (https://freegamesprites.com) — each asset page
carries the CC0 license in its metadata. The PNGs here are the original
256×256 sprites downscaled to 128×128 (nearest, integer 2×) for the game's
≤44px body render; dwarven-mine-worker is the source's native 96×96.

  beggar-boy-tin-cup.png          https://freegamesprites.com/en/assets/beggar-boy-tin-cup
  cybernetic-arm-technician.png   https://freegamesprites.com/en/assets/cybernetic-arm-technician
  deep-mole.png                 original — authored in-repo (16×16 pixel grid scaled 8× to 128×128,
                                scripts/generate-deep-mole-sprite.mjs), dedicated to the public domain
  dwarven-mine-worker.png         https://freegamesprites.com/en/assets/dwarven-mine-worker
